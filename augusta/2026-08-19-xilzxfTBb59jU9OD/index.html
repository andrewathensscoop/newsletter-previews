🦪 A James Beard Semifinalist's Oyster Bar in Surrey Center

At 491 Highland Avenue in Surrey Center, Abel Brown Southern Kitchen & Oyster Bar is chef-owner Todd Schafer's modern Southern seafood spot. Schafer was a 2026 James Beard semifinalist for Best Chef: Southeast.

The polished, comfortable room centers on oysters from the raw bar and an ingredient-driven menu that changes with the season. The restaurant's name comes from the traditional sea shanty "Abel Brown," a fitting nod for a place built around seafood and Southern roots.

🪖 AU Opens College-Credit Path for Army Cyber Training

The Gist: Augusta University and the Army Cyber Center formed a partnership that can convert tactical military courses into graduate-level college credit. The agreement was highlighted during TechNet, which brought military, government, university and cyber-industry leaders to downtown Augusta.

Why It Matters: Service members can turn training they already completed into faster progress toward a degree, strengthening Augusta's pipeline of military and civilian cyber talent.

What's Next: TechNet runs through Aug. 20 in downtown Augusta.

Read more here.

🛠️ Free Trade Program Builds Skills and a Tiny House

The Gist: Construction Ready and the Home Builders Institute are running a free eight-week pre-apprenticeship at Augusta Technical College through Sept. 9. Adult students are building a tiny house while learning carpentry and tool skills, then meeting employers before graduation.

Why It Matters: More Georgians are retiring from the trades than entering them, so the class connects local adults with no-cost training and contractors trying to fill the worker gap.

What's Next: A hiring fair is set for Sept. 8, followed by graduation and expected job-placement announcements Sept. 9.

Read more here.

🎣 Langley Pond Pier Work Resumes After Delay

The Gist: Crews are back on the new fishing pier at Langley Pond Park after materials delays paused the final phase earlier this year. A major dam leak closed the park in 2014, and the gates reopened in 2022 as Aiken County rebuilt the property step by step.

Why It Matters: The pier restores a long-awaited public fishing amenity at one of Aiken County's best-known outdoor gathering places.

What's Next: The parks department hopes to finish the fishing pier by January.

Read more here.

🌡️ Extreme Heat Pushes More Residents Into Shelters

The Gist: Augusta shelters report more people seeking relief as temperatures climb. The Love Center is seeing 50 to 100 visitors a day, while the Salvation Army's 110-bed Center of Hope is mostly occupied by families and currently houses 22 children.

Why It Matters: Extreme heat is forcing even people who usually stay outside to seek shelter, putting added pressure on limited family beds and daytime relief space.

What's Next: Center of Hope moved check-in from 4 p.m. to 1:30 p.m., and Augusta's community-center cooling sites remain open during regular hours.

Read more here.

🦝 Rabid Raccoon Confirmed at Blanchard Woods Park

The Gist: A raccoon that bit someone Aug. 14 at Blanchard Woods Park in Evans tested positive for rabies. Columbia County Animal Services captured the animal, officials confirmed the result Wednesday, and the bite victim has started rabies treatment.

Why It Matters: The confirmed exposure at a popular park is a direct reminder to keep children and pets away from wild animals and keep pet vaccinations current.

What's Next: Any contact between a person or pet and a wild animal should be reported to the Columbia County Health Department or Animal Services.
