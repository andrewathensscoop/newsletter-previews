🥩 Hand-Cut Steaks on Washington Road Since 1986

At I-20 and Washington Road, TBonz Steakhouse has served Augusta since 1986. Certified Angus Beef steaks are hand-cut in-house, and the menu stretches from wings and baby back ribs to seafood and Tommy's Texas Cheese Fries.

The room is casual and family-friendly, with space for groups, a full-service bar, and lunch-through-late-night hours. More than two generations in, TBonz still works with local schools, charities, and community events.

🧒 South Augusta Club Fills All 250 Spots

The Gist: The South Augusta Boys & Girls Club has filled all 250 spots and now has a waiting list. Across the organization, its clubs serve 1,183 children, while Richmond County has about 30,000 school-age kids.

Why It Matters: The waitlist shows how tight after-school capacity already is as the South Augusta YMCA prepares to close, leaving working families with fewer nearby options.

What's Next: The South Augusta YMCA is scheduled to close its doors Sept. 14.

🦝 Aiken Rabies Cases Double as Treatment Tops $10K

The Gist: Aiken County has confirmed eight rabies cases this year, more than double last year's total, after two raccoons and a horse tested positive across the CSRA in one week. Post-exposure treatment can exceed $10,000 without insurance, and individual shots cost more than $400 before emergency-room fees.

Why It Matters: Aiken residents may have to travel to Augusta for treatment, and even insured patients can face thousands of dollars in out-of-pocket costs.

What's Next: Anyone with a possible exposure should contact South Carolina public-health officials, who investigate cases and can direct uninsured or underinsured patients to assistance programs.

🏠 Fort Gordon Sets Sept. 1 Housing Inspection Deadline

The Gist: Fort Gordon will conduct independent, hour-long inspections of privatized housing Sept. 8 through Sept. 18. Inspectors will check smoke detectors, structural issues and other safety concerns, not housekeeping or cleanliness.

Why It Matters: Military families have reported mold, leaks and serious maintenance problems for years, so the inspections provide a direct check on current living conditions.

What's Next: Residents have until Sept. 1 to book appointments. If 296 homes are not scheduled, inspectors will randomly select those units and assign time slots.

Read more here.

🚧 $6.15M in Roadwork Targets Three Richmond Routes

The Gist: GDOT awarded E.R. Snell three contracts totaling $6.15 million: $2.18 million for lane changes at I-520 Exit 7, $2.74 million to resurface more than four miles of Mike Padgett Highway and $1.23 million to resurface about two miles of State Route 88.

Why It Matters: The work will reshape a busy Bobby Jones interchange and renew two major South Richmond County corridors used by commuters, trucks and nearby neighborhoods.

What's Next: The resurfacing projects are due June 30, 2027, and the Exit 7 work is due Oct. 31, 2027.

Read more here.

🏕️ $100K Gift Expands Camp Tanglewood

The Gist: Georgia Power is giving $100,000 to the Girl Scouts of Historic Georgia's Trails to Tomorrow campaign for Camp Tanglewood. Plans call for modernized facilities, expanded program space and a new DreamLab where girls can create, collaborate and build projects.

Why It Matters: The investment adds capacity and updated programming at a longtime local camp that also received $50,000 in June for repairs after Hurricane Helene.

What's Next: The gift presentation is set for Sept. 11 at 2 p.m. at Camp Tanglewood, followed by a tour and a look at the property's long-term plans.
