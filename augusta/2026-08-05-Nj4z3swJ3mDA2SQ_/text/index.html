🏘️ $68K Restores Maps and Public Input to Zoning Rewrite

🏘️ $68K Restores Maps and Public Input to Zoning Rewrite

The Gist: Augusta commissioners voted 7-3 Tuesday to approve up to $68,368 for White & Smith LLC to finish a comprehensive zoning-code update, the city's first since 1963. The amendment restores two key maps, two public meetings, a survey and outreach work cut when the original contract was reduced.

Why It Matters: The new code will govern where and how Augusta grows, including a planned requirement to notify nearby homeowners about proposed rezonings.

What's Next: Community workshops are planned for August, followed by a public draft in September. Adoption hearings are scheduled for Nov. 2 before the Planning Commission and Nov. 17 before the Augusta Commission.

Read more here.

🏗️ North Augusta Rejects 1,800-Home Annexation

🏗️ North Augusta Rejects 1,800-Home Annexation

The Gist: North Augusta City Council voted 3-1 Monday against annexing the Kellogg Tract, several parcels north of Belvedere Clearwater Road and west of I-520. The mixed-use proposal could bring nearly 1,800 homes and commercial space, with more than 80 acres protected as wetlands.

Why It Matters: Residents and council members raised traffic, stormwater, school-capacity and sewer concerns as western Aiken County grows.

What's Next: Two related development votes were canceled, but Superintendent Colen Lindell said the project could still proceed under Aiken County jurisdiction.

Read more here.

🏛️ Kendrick Sets Four Augusta Listening Sessions

🏛️ Kendrick Sets Four Augusta Listening Sessions

The Gist: Mayor-elect Steven Kendrick named a transition team led by co-chairs Jordan Johnson and Sue Parr. Six committees will develop recommendations for his first 90 days and a 12-month governing framework, with findings due in December.

Why It Matters: The group will help set Augusta's priorities on economic development, finance, government reform, city operations, community reinvestment and public engagement.

What's Next: Public sessions begin at 6 p.m. Aug. 17 at Diamond Lakes, Aug. 20 at Henry Brigham, Aug. 24 at Julian Smith Casino and Aug. 27 at Warren Road.

🍎 Free Meals Cover 45 Richmond County Schools

🍎 Free Meals Cover 45 Richmond County Schools

The Gist: Students at 45 Richmond County schools will receive breakfast and lunch at no charge during the 2026-27 school year. The district is using the federal Community Eligibility Provision through the National School Lunch and School Breakfast programs.

Why It Matters: Families at participating schools will not pay for two daily meals or submit household meal applications, removing a recurring cost and paperwork hurdle.

Read more here.

💧 Aiken Wastewater Upgrade Heads to November Ballot

💧 Aiken Wastewater Upgrade Heads to November Ballot

The Gist: Aiken County Council voted unanimously Tuesday to advance upgrades to its wastewater treatment plant, which the county says is already at capacity. The project is intended to make room for more homes and other development across Aiken County.

Why It Matters: With the current plant already at capacity, the vote will shape how much future residential and commercial growth the sewer system can support.

What's Next: The proposal is not final. Voters will decide on the wastewater improvements during the November general election.
