🍽️ A Martinez Fine-Dining Mainstay Since 1987

At 106-A Davis Road in Martinez, Cadwalladers Cafe has served upscale American dinners since 1987. The weekly changing menu moves between seafood, steaks, bison, lamb, seasonal salads, and house-made desserts.

Chef Eric Lindsay, part of the restaurant since 2008, now runs the kitchen while owner John Donner carries on the Waller family's tradition. Expect business-casual dining, a full bar, and one of the CSRA's largest whiskey selections.

🏗️ Google Project Puts Tax Relief on November Ballot

The Gist: Google will occupy two data center complexes near Appling covering 33 buildings and 3,500 acres, Columbia County Manager Scott Johnson said. A November referendum will ask voters whether the county may use project revenue for property-tax relief, with credits possible in 2027.

Why It Matters: The county says homestead bills could eventually fall to zero, while neighbors still face years of construction and questions about the sites' local impact.

What's Next: Voters decide in November. Full elimination could not happen before 2028 and would also require a state constitutional amendment.

Read more here.

🔨 Rally Augusta Seeks $1M to Keep Rebuilding

The Gist: Rally Augusta says it has completed 197 Hurricane Helene recovery cases and 135 major construction projects since February 2025. Its work covers Richmond, Columbia and McDuffie counties, where 65.9% of homeowners served are at least 60 and all were uninsured or underinsured.

Why It Matters: Blue tarps and condemned homes remain nearly two years after the storm, especially for older residents who cannot fund or manage major repairs.

What's Next: Rally Augusta wants $1 million more for materials before Mennonite teams are expected to return in September or October. Homeowners still needing help can register through ngumc.org/disasterassistance.

Read more here.

🏥 Wellstar Cuts 761 Jobs Across Georgia

The Gist: Wellstar Health System is eliminating 761 corporate, shared-services and administrative positions, about 2% of its workforce of more than 35,000. The system says doctors, nurses and other frontline clinical roles are not included.

Why It Matters: Wellstar runs MCG Health in Augusta and is preparing to open Columbia County's first hospital, but it has not disclosed how many local employees are affected.

Read more here.

🚧 Barton Chapel Closure Runs Through Aug. 14

The Gist: Barton Chapel Road is closed to through traffic between Gordon Highway and Wrightsboro Road from July 27 through Aug. 14 for new underground piping. The marked detour follows Gordon Highway, North Leg Road and Wrightsboro Road.

Why It Matters: The shutdown cuts a key South Augusta connection during the back-to-school rush, so commuters and families should expect extra travel time.

What's Next: Traffic control crews will keep local access and detours in place until the scheduled Aug. 14 end date.

Read more here.

🏀 Cross Creek Gets Its Class 3A Championship Rings

The Gist: Cross Creek's basketball team received rings for the Class 3A state championship it won in March. The Razorbacks beat Harlem 56-51 after scoring 24 points in the fourth quarter, their 19th straight victory.

Why It Matters: The championship gives South Augusta a state title and closes the nine-year Cross Creek coaching tenure of Lawrence Kelley, now Richmond County athletic director.
