💰 Google Deal Puts $40M a Year Toward Tax Relief

💰 Google Deal Puts $40M a Year Toward Tax Relief

The Gist: Columbia County and its Development Authority approved an agreement tied to Google's planned $17 billion data center investment. The county is set to receive $40 million a year for seven years, while the authority would receive about $5 million annually.

Why It Matters: The county currently collects about $25 million a year in homestead property taxes, so the payments could fund substantial homeowner relief and other local services.

What's Next: Voters will decide on the county's homestead tax relief mechanism in November. The first Google payment is expected late this year or early next year.

🏢 160-Plus Apartment Violations Spur Licensing Rewrite

🏢 160-Plus Apartment Violations Spur Licensing Rewrite

The Gist: Augusta inspectors found 66 violations at Brook Landing Apartments and 95 at Pace Corners. Brook Landing received a business license before an inspection, and the city's licensing, code enforcement, permitting and tax systems do not currently share property information.

Why It Matters: The disconnect allowed troubled apartment properties to operate without the checks meant to protect tenants and collect required occupational taxes.

What's Next: Licensing Director Adleasia Cameron is drafting a rule that would require an inspection before a license is issued. It needs mayoral and commission approval, and a connected Tyler software system could follow within 45 to 60 days of sign-off.

🏫 School Board Cuts Millage Rate by 0.488

🏫 School Board Cuts Millage Rate by 0.488

The Gist: The Richmond County Board of Education lowered the school millage rate from 18.74 to 18.252 for the next budget year, a reduction of 0.488 mills.

Why It Matters: School taxes make up a major share of local property bills. The lower rate will offset some pressure from rising assessments, though each bill still depends on taxable value and exemptions.

✈️ Airport Closes 350 Parking Spaces for Three Months

✈️ Airport Closes 350 Parking Spaces for Three Months

The Gist: Augusta Regional Airport closed Long-Term Lot A for about three months, temporarily cutting primary parking capacity by roughly 350 spaces. The lot will return with about 50 additional spaces, and the airport plans to add about 250 public spaces over two years.

Why It Matters: Passengers could face full lots and longer walks during peak periods, so the airport is urging travelers to allow extra time or arrange a ride to the terminal.

What's Next: Lot A is expected to reopen after the three-month project. Until then, Lots B, C and D, the credit card lot and discounted QR-code overflow parking remain available.

Read more here.

🏥 Aiken Health Merger Would Serve 100K Patients

🏥 Aiken Health Merger Would Serve 100K Patients

The Gist: HopeHealth and Rural Health Services plan to merge into a nonprofit provider serving about 100,000 patients across 24 clinical sites. Both organizations said primary care will continue at their Aiken County facilities.

Why It Matters: The combined system would become one of the region's largest community-based providers and could broaden primary and specialty care for underserved patients.

What's Next: The merger is awaiting formal approval from the federal Health Resources and Services Administration.
