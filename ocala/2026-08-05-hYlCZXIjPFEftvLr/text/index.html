🗳️ Four Republicans Seek Zalak's Commission Seat

🗳️ Four Republicans Seek Zalak's Commission Seat

The Gist: Randall Alvord, Jeff Bairstow, Wanda Wimberly Lasher, and Sherri Meadows are running for Marion County Commission District 4 after 16-year incumbent Carl Zalak declined another term. All four have centered the race on roads, growth, rural land, and infrastructure.

Why It Matters: The primary winner will face no general-election opponent, making this vote decisive for a seat that shapes land use, transportation, services, and the county budget.

What's Next: Vote-by-mail requests are due Aug. 6 at 5 p.m. Early voting runs Aug. 8-15, and the primary is Aug. 18.

🏘️ Ocala Weighs 30-Year Housing Loans

🏘️ Ocala Weighs 30-Year Housing Loans

The Gist: Ocala is considering Ordinance 2026-27, which would split Housing Incentive Fund financing into short-term loans of 10 years or less and long-term loans of up to 30 years. Long-term loans would require another funding source and carry interest between half of the U.S. prime rate and the full prime rate.

Why It Matters: The change could make city financing compatible with state and federal programs and help more affordable homes get built or preserved.

What's Next: City Council's second and final reading is scheduled for Aug. 18.

🎭 The Nest Plans Five Food Stalls and Amphitheater

🎭 The Nest Plans Five Food Stalls and Amphitheater

The Gist: Construction is advancing at The Nest in Calesa Township near FAST and Ina A. Colen Academy. Plans call for an outdoor amphitheater, beer garden, taproom, dog park, playground, pickleball and sand volleyball, plus The Faregrounds with five permanent 250-square-foot food-and-drink buildings.

Why It Matters: The venue will be open to the public, adding dining, recreation, and event space to fast-growing southwest Ocala.

What's Next: The developer is seeking county approval for revised site plans. The Marion County Development Review Committee next meets Aug. 10 at 9 a.m.

🏺 Pine Avenue Mall Reunites Diggers Vendors

🏺 Pine Avenue Mall Reunites Diggers Vendors

The Gist: Ken Kisner and Richard Rice are building an 8,000-square-foot Pine Avenue Antique Mall at 510 S. Pine Ave. The shop will bring many former Diggers vendors back together after the longtime mall closed July 31 following more than a decade in business.

Why It Matters: The move keeps longtime local vendors in business and preserves a piece of Ocala's antique-shopping scene.

What's Next: Pine Avenue Antique Mall plans to open Sept. 1.

🥫 Interfaith Pantry Calls for Food Drives

🥫 Interfaith Pantry Calls for Food Drives

The Gist: Interfaith Emergency Services says its pantry shelves are depleted as back-to-school demand rises. It is asking businesses, churches, civic groups, and residents for food drives and nonperishables including canned meats, vegetables, cereal, pasta, rice, peanut butter, and jelly.

Why It Matters: The pantry supplies emergency meals to Marion County families and also supports the nonprofit's Food 4 Kids weekend meal program.

What's Next: Groups can arrange a drive with Food Distribution Manager Maxi Rodriguez at 352-622-4204. Donations can be delivered to 421 NW First St. in Ocala.
