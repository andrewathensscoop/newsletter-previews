East Ocala's Wolfy's Reopens Under New Ownership

At 2159 E. Silver Springs Boulevard, The New Wolfy's brings a longtime east Ocala diner back under new ownership from the team behind Scrambles Cafe, following a full renovation and a late-June reopening.

The dining room, kitchen, and restrooms were rebuilt, while the familiar comfort-food format stayed put. The expanded menu covers breakfast, lunch, and dinner, and several longtime employees came back after the renovation.

🗳️ Crimi and Bairstow Win Commission Primaries

The Gist: Mike Crimi defeated Matthew Cretul and Brien Weidemiller in the Republican primary for Marion County Commission District 2. Jeff Bairstow topped Randall Alvord, Wanda Wimberly Lasher, and Sherri Meadows in District 4, where he received 39.02% of the vote.

Why It Matters: The races fill seats held for 16 years by Kathy Bryant and Carl Zalak on the five-member board that decides county zoning, roads, public-safety funding, and taxes.

What's Next: Crimi faces write-in Mark Okus, while Bairstow faces write-in Leonard Racioppi, in the Nov. 3 general election.

💰 Ocala Proposes $1.27 Billion Budget

The Gist: Ocala's tentative 2026-27 budget totals $1.27 billion, with 51% earmarked for reserves and 25% for operations. The proposed 6.6177 millage rate is unchanged, but a home with $250,000 in taxable value would owe about $1,654.43 in city property tax.

Why It Matters: The plan includes $178.6 million for salaries and benefits, $71.2 million across 48 capital projects, and a $1 monthly sanitation increase in October.

What's Next: The first public hearing is Sept. 9 at 5 p.m. in City Council Chambers. The final hearing and council vote are Sept. 15.

Read more here.

🌿 County Pauses Kratom Ban as DEA Weighs 7-OH

The Gist: Marion County commissioners paused work on a local kratom ordinance Aug. 18 after hearing from health advocates, businesses, and County Attorney Matthew Minter. Minter said enforcing a rule aimed only at concentrated 7-OH would require local laboratory testing.

Why It Matters: Florida bars kratom sales to people under 21 and treats products with more than 400 ppm of 7-OH as Schedule I. A countywide ban could reach beyond concentrated products to traditional whole-leaf kratom.

What's Next: Commissioners plan to track the DEA process and coordinate with Ocala before returning to a local ordinance. No return date was set.

🔎 Ocala Picks Vincent Iovino as Internal Auditor

The Gist: The Ocala City Council voted 4-0 on Aug. 11 to promote IT auditor Vincent Iovino to internal auditor. His salary still must be negotiated, but city staff said it will likely fall between $113,000 and $120,000.

Why It Matters: The auditor reports directly to the council and reviews city finances for waste, fraud, and noncompliance. The office is down to two auditors after Randall Bridgeman died May 10.

What's Next: Iovino plans to roll out an electronic system within his first 90 days so council members and city staff can monitor audit progress in real time.

Read more here.

✈️ Blue Angels Headline Planned 2027 Ocala Air Show

The Gist: Wings Over Ocala is planned as a three-day festival at Ocala International Airport from April 30 through May 2, 2027, headlined by the U.S. Navy Blue Angels. Under the proposed agreement, the city would cover $38,175 in police, fire, and EMS support from the Airport Fund.

Why It Matters: County records estimate about $4.5 million in business sales and commit $100,000 per event year in tourism support.

What's Next: The full event website and ticket sales are scheduled to go live Sept. 2.
