🚒 Records Flag 146 Out-of-Service Hydrants

🚒 Records Flag 146 Out-of-Service Hydrants

The Gist: A July 20 Charleston Water System list obtained by Live 5 showed 146 of its more than 11,000 hydrants damaged or otherwise out of service. The utility checks hydrants every three years and prioritizes a 24- to 48-hour repair when no working hydrant is within roughly 500 feet.

Why It Matters: Crews can adapt and use nearby hydrants, but a reliable first water source matters when firefighters arrive and seconds count.

📚 CCSD Sets 75.8% Reading Goal

📚 CCSD Sets 75.8% Reading Goal

The Gist: The Charleston County school board approved academic goals for 2027 through 2031, including a target of 75.8% of students reading at or above grade level by 2031. The roadmap also covers math, science, college and career readiness, and school climate.

Why It Matters: The benchmarks will shape school plans and evaluations for principals and teachers, with added attention to students facing poverty, disabilities or language barriers.

What's Next: The goals take effect in 2027 and will guide district work through 2031.

Read more here.

🏘️ $60M Robert Mills Manor Renovation Advances

🏘️ $60M Robert Mills Manor Renovation Advances

The Gist: The Charleston Housing Authority plans a $60 million renovation of 87-year-old Robert Mills Manor, covering exterior work and repairs inside its 217 units. Charleston County is considering a 100% property tax reduction for 44 years to make the project financially viable.

Why It Matters: Keeping the complex in service would preserve 217 affordable homes as the county tries to add tens of thousands more units.

What's Next: The incentive needs three County Council approvals, with a final vote expected by the end of September.

🚑 Dorchester Doubles Community Paramedic Team

🚑 Dorchester Doubles Community Paramedic Team

The Gist: Dorchester County is adding a second community paramedic after Angelina Johnson served more than 1,200 residents in the program's first year. She connected 200 people with primary care, completed 134 post-overdose visits and helped 24 people enter treatment or recovery.

Why It Matters: The program redirects non-emergency needs away from ambulances while linking residents with medical equipment, food, housing and routine care.

What's Next: Hiring begins this fall, and the second paramedic is expected to start in January.

Read more here.

🚦 $5.5M Savannah Highway Upgrade Nears Vote

🚦 $5.5M Savannah Highway Upgrade Nears Vote

The Gist: Charleston County is moving a $5.5 million safety and traffic project toward construction at Savannah Highway's intersections with Wappoo Road and Dupont Road/Stinson Drive. Plans add turn lanes, sidewalks and marked crosswalks, including a connection between the West Ashley Greenway and Bikeway.

Why It Matters: The work targets two congested crossings used by drivers, cyclists and pedestrians and will let the county shorten some signal cycles.

What's Next: County Council is scheduled to consider final approval Aug. 27. If approved, construction could begin in late October or early November and finish by the end of 2027.
