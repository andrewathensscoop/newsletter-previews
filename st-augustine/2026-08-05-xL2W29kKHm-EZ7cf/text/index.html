🚶 County Launches Crosswalk Safety Program

🚶 County Launches Crosswalk Safety Program

The Gist: St. Johns County became the first Northeast Florida county to adopt Best Foot Forward, a program combining driver education, high-visibility enforcement, engineering changes and data collection. The County Commission endorsed the rollout July 21, and the program now operates in 11 Florida counties.

Why It Matters: The effort targets whether drivers stop for people in marked crosswalks, a basic safety issue as county roads grow busier.

What's Next: Baseline data collection has started at selected crosswalks and will guide future enforcement, education and infrastructure work.

📄 Five Clerk Services Move to Lewis Speedway

📄 Five Clerk Services Move to Lewis Speedway

The Gist: The St. Johns County Clerk opened a Public Service Center on August 3 at 4040 Lewis Speedway in St. Augustine. Recording, marriage, passport, Value Adjustment Board and public-record search services moved across the parking lot from the courthouse.

Why It Matters: Closer parking and more service windows should shorten routine visits without disrupting court operations at the Richard O. Watson Judicial Center.

What's Next: The new center is open Monday through Friday from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.; jury duty, traffic payments and court proceedings remain at 4010 Lewis Speedway.

🛝 Lighthouse Park Gets Five-Week ADA Overhaul

🛝 Lighthouse Park Gets Five-Week ADA Overhaul

The Gist: St. Augustine will close Lighthouse Park at 440 Ocean Vista Drive on August 12 for an approximately five-week renovation. Crews will remove the existing playground and install ADA-compliant equipment, sidewalks, handrails and new landscaping.

Why It Matters: The project replaces an aging neighborhood playground with a more accessible space, but the entire park will be unavailable during construction.

What's Next: The city expects the park to reopen September 16, weather and unforeseen delays permitting.

🥫 Elkton Resource Center Opens With Weekly Groceries

🥫 Elkton Resource Center Opens With Weekly Groceries

The Gist: The S.E.A. Community Help Resource Center opened a permanent nonprofit hub at 6408 Armstrong Road in Elkton. It offers food, clothing, school supplies and support services for residents of Spuds, Elkton, Armstrong and nearby rural communities.

Why It Matters: A permanent site brings basic assistance closer to families who previously relied on mobile or church-based outreach and may face transportation barriers.

What's Next: Weekly grocery distributions are scheduled every Friday from 9 a.m. to noon.

Read more here.

📚 Summer Reading Draws 17,500 Library Visitors

📚 Summer Reading Draws 17,500 Library Visitors

The Gist: St. Johns County libraries hosted more than 200 summer reading events from May 31 through July 25, drawing over 17,500 visitors to six branches and bookmobile stops. Participants earned more than 8,000 prizes through free reading logs.

Why It Matters: The turnout shows strong demand for free literacy programs available through neighborhood branches and the county bookmobile.
