🥪 Spanish-Cuban cooking on St. George since 1983

At 98 St. George Street, Columbia Restaurant has served Spanish and Cuban family recipes since Fourth of July weekend in 1983. The menu includes Cuban sandwiches, black beans and rice, paella, and arroz con pollo.

Hundreds of hand-painted tiles, artwork, and a Spanish-style fountain fill the two-story dining rooms. The St. Augustine location belongs to the Hernandez/Gonzmart family's Florida restaurant group, which began in Tampa in 1905.

🗳️ Four local races settled in Tuesday's primary

The Gist: Unofficial Aug. 18 results gave Krista Keating-Joseph 47.24% in County Commission District 4, Martin Pyszczymuka 35.46% in District 2, Ashleigh Barnes 60.45% for St. Augustine Commission Seat 4, and Kevin Allen 52.22% for School Board District 1.

Why It Matters: Those four races are complete, setting new leadership for county growth decisions, city policy, and the school district.

What's Next: School Board District 4 moves to a Nov. 3 runoff between Tammie McClafferty, who received 46.17%, and Jillian Tozer, who received 27.92%.

🌿 $100M conservation bond goes to November ballot

The Gist: St. Johns County commissioners voted 3-2 on Aug. 18 to send voters a bond of up to $100 million for the Land Acquisition and Management Program, which now receives $500,000 a year. Repayment could add up to $15 annually per $100,000 of taxable property value.

Why It Matters: The money could help the county move faster on willing-seller land that protects drinking water, wildlife habitat, farms, forests, coastlines, and flood storage.

What's Next: Voters will decide the bond referendum in the Nov. 3 general election.

Read more here.

🏘️ West Augustine opens 92 affordable apartments

The Gist: Ability Housing completed the $26.5 million Village of New Augustine at 280 N. Volusia St. The 92 apartments serve households earning up to 60% of area median income, with current rents from about $634 for a one-bedroom to $1,599 for a three-bedroom.

Why It Matters: The project adds below-market housing near downtown jobs in a fast-growing county where many service workers commute long distances.

What's Next: Leasing continues after the community opened 30% to 40% leased.

Read more here.

💧 Extreme water shortage order stays in force

The Gist: St. Johns County remained under a Phase III Extreme Water Shortage Order as of Aug. 13. The county ended July about 8.3 inches below its 12-month rainfall average, with conditions ranging from moderate to severe drought despite recent afternoon storms.

Why It Matters: Outdoor irrigation can account for 50% to 60% of household water use, so the active restrictions directly affect homes, businesses, and golf courses.

What's Next: No end date is set. The water district will lift the order only after regional rainfall, groundwater, and river conditions improve.

Read more here.

🧾 New business licenses pause for September

The Gist: The City of St. Augustine will issue no new business licenses during September. Existing licenses must be renewed by Wednesday, Sept. 30.

Why It Matters: The monthlong pause can delay new storefronts and service businesses, while current operators still face the normal renewal deadline.

What's Next: New license issuance resumes after Oct. 1. The city's Business Licensing Office is taking questions at 904-825-1037.
