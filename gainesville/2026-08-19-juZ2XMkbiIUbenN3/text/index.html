🗳️ Ward wins final term as Gainesville mayor

🗳️ Ward wins final term as Gainesville mayor

The Gist: Mayor Harvey Ward won nearly 80% of the vote for a fourth and final City Commission term. Casey Willits kept District 3 with 54%, Nathan Crabbe won the open District 2 seat with 62% of 7,593 votes, and Bryan Eastman was unopposed in District 4.

Why It Matters: The results settle four commission seats that will shape Gainesville decisions on housing, taxes, infrastructure and the continuing GRU governance fight.

What's Next: Ward, Willits, Crabbe and Eastman will be sworn in for four-year terms in January 2027.

🏫 Rockwell reelected as school board race heads to runoff

🏫 Rockwell reelected as school board race heads to runoff

The Gist: Sarah Rockwell won another term in Alachua County School Board District 3 with 57%, or 23,729 votes. In District 1, teacher Hunter Miller received 46% and incumbent Janine Plavac received 36%, sending both to the general election; Jancie Vinson won District 5 unopposed.

Why It Matters: The runoff will decide whether Plavac, a DeSantis appointee, or a Bishop Middle math teacher takes the District 1 seat.

What's Next: Miller and Plavac will face each other in the Nov. 3 general election.

🛣️ County roads face $15.6 million annual funding gap

🛣️ County roads face $15.6 million annual funding gap

The Gist: Alachua County spends about $26 million a year on its 700-mile road network but now estimates it needs $41.6 million annually just to hold the average pavement score at 60 out of 100. The county’s road backlog stood at $408 million in 2022.

Why It Matters: Most residential roads are outside the current 10-year project list, leaving more drivers with worsening pavement and recurring potholes despite four repair crews.

What's Next: Commissioners directed staff to expand maintenance with new hires and contractors. Residents can report road problems at rbmaintenance@alachuacounty.gov.

Read more here.

🚸 Metcalfe reviews dismissal after first grader walks two miles

🚸 Metcalfe reviews dismissal after first grader walks two miles

The Gist: A 6-year-old Metcalfe Elementary student was mistakenly dismissed Aug. 11 and walked roughly two miles before a school cafeteria worker found him near Mount Carmel Church. His grandmother had arrived for pickup and was initially told he was in an on-campus program.

Why It Matters: The incident exposed a serious dismissal and student-accountability failure during the first week of school.

What's Next: The principal apologized and said the school is reviewing dismissal procedures and will make changes needed to prevent another incident.

🎬 Mac’s Drive Thru documentary lands at Netflix

🎬 Mac’s Drive Thru documentary lands at Netflix

The Gist: Netflix acquired “One Last Order,” a short documentary following Sighle Williams through her final shift after 37 years at Mac’s Drive Thru. The film won top jury or audience honors at festivals in Aspen, Miami and Nantucket this year.

Why It Matters: The acquisition gives a Gainesville institution and one of its longtime employees a national audience after a standout festival run.
