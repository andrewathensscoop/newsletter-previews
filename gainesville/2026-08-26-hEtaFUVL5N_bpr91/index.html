🔥 Wood-fired pizza and hand-rolled bagels on SW 4th

At 307 SW 4th Avenue, Humble Wood Fire serves wood-fired pizza and hand-rolled bagels. The Gainesville business dates to 2012, and its mobile setup still handles food-truck stops and catered events around town.

Everything starts from scratch, from a handmade 800-degree oven to organic bagels rolled each morning. The catering crew brings the oven to weddings, parties, and events, while the bagel shop opens daily at 7 a.m.

🏘️ Newberry approves 500-home CountryWay II

The Gist: The Newberry City Commission unanimously gave final rezoning approval Aug. 24 to Norfleet Construction Group's 500-home CountryWay II project on 160 acres beside CountryWay TownSquare. The plan allows up to 636 units, 3,500-square-foot minimum lots and a new collector road to State Road 26.

Why It Matters: CountryWay II pushes Newberry past 2,000 homes approved this year, adding to a fast-growing pipeline that includes two other large developments.

🤝 County keeps housing-first model in $350,000 outreach

The Gist: Alachua County commissioners unanimously directed staff to keep a $350,000 homeless street-outreach bid aligned with the county's housing-first model while still meeting new federal treatment-service requirements. The money covers a third year of outreach.

Why It Matters: The county receives more than $1 million in federal homeless-services funding, and a contract delay could leave street outreach unfunded Oct. 1.

What's Next: The current contract ends Sept. 30, and the replacement is expected to be in place Oct. 1.

Read more here.

🏫 School Board launches $47,200 superintendent search

The Gist: The Alachua County School Board unanimously hired McPherson & Jacobson for a $47,200 national superintendent search. The firm's five-phase process will run eight to 12 weeks and includes stakeholder input, recruiting, background checks and a two-year replacement guarantee.

Why It Matters: Alachua County Public Schools has operated without a permanent superintendent for nearly two years.

What's Next: The firm will meet with the board at a Sept. 9 workshop, with a permanent hire targeted by the end of 2026.

Read more here.

💸 Gainesville two-bedroom rent benchmark climbs 54%

The Gist: HUD's fair-market rent for a two-bedroom in the Gainesville metro climbed from $971 in 2020 to $1,493 in 2026, a 54% increase. UF's cheapest traditional double rose from $3,556 per semester last year to $3,929 this year.

Why It Matters: With UF estimating $25,830 for a full in-state undergraduate year, students report taking extra jobs, sharing leases and cooking to close the gap.

What's Next: UF trustees have approved combined 6.5% yearly housing increases through fiscal 2030, though rates are still reviewed annually.

Read more here.

🌲 Black Lake Preserve opens 287 acres in Melrose

The Gist: Alachua County opened the 287-acre Black Lake Preserve on Aug. 21 after buying the land for $2.3 million in 2020 with Wild Spaces Public Places surtax money. Visitors get a 500-foot accessible paved path and more than three miles of hiking trails.

Why It Matters: Land once planned for housing now protects wetlands that store and filter rainwater while giving the Melrose area a nearby public nature park.

What's Next: Starting Oct. 1, the preserve's daily closing time shifts from 8 p.m. to 6 p.m.
